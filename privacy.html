Black Tide privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Black Tide is a free game with optional ads and optional purchases. This page explains what data the game collects, why, who handles it, how long it's kept and how you can limit it.

What the game collects

Gameplay events. To understand how the game plays (where players get stuck, whether they come back, what they buy), the game sends events about what you do in it: for example, starting and finishing a battle, upgrading your ship, changing gear, sending out your fleet, opening the Shop, watching an ad or changing a setting. They carry details such as the zone, the result, how long a battle took, your ship's power, how your shots went, which screens you spent time on, your in-game currency, your settings, your answers to the game's tracking and notification prompts, and how smoothly the game ran (frame rate, load times, graphics memory). Each event carries a random player ID (its start is your Player ID, at the bottom of Settings) and a random ID for this install, plus your device model, iOS version and the game's version.

Purchases. Apple handles every payment: we never see your card details, your name or your Apple Account. When you buy something, the game sends an event with what you bought, the price and currency, the App Store country and Apple's transaction number, tied to the same random IDs.

The game does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any free text, and the random IDs are not your device's advertising identifier. Your IP address is not stored with the gameplay events and is not used to work out where you are. The player ID is made when you first install the game and is kept with your saved game, so if you use iCloud it comes back with your progress when you reinstall or move to a new phone.

Ads. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. When an ad loads or plays, AdMob collects information about your device and the ad (for example, an advertising identifier if you allow tracking, your IP address and the rough location it gives, whether the ad was shown or tapped, and crash and performance data) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your saved game. If you use iCloud, your progress and purchases are backed up to your own iCloud account so they come back on a new phone, with your player ID. Apple stores them; we can't see them.

Who handles it

  • PostHog stores the gameplay and purchase events for us (in the United States) and only uses them to provide the analytics service to us.
  • Google AdMob serves the ads and handles ad data under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Apple handles payments and iCloud under Apple's own privacy policy.

We don't sell the gameplay or purchase events or share them with anyone else.

How long it's kept

Gameplay and purchase events are kept for up to two years, then deleted. Ad data is kept by Google under its own policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether Black Tide may track you for ads. If you say no (or later turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • Personalized ads and "Do not sell or share". Where the law gives you these choices, the game shows Google's consent message, and your answer is applied to every ad. Change it any time from Settings → Ad choices in the game.
  • Your events. To get a copy of the events tied to your install, or have them deleted, email us at the address below with your Player ID: it's at the bottom of Settings in the game, and a tap copies it. The events aren't tied to your name or any account, so the Player ID is the only way we can find yours.
  • Do Not Track. The game has no web browser, so it doesn't receive browser "Do Not Track" signals. This website has no tracking or cookies.

Children

Black Tide is rated 13+ and is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used the game, email us their Player ID (at the bottom of Settings) and we'll delete the events tied to it.

Changes and contact
