Black Tide privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Black Tide is a free game paid for by optional ads. This page explains what data the game collects, why, and how you can limit it.

What the game collects

Gameplay events. To understand how the game plays (where players get stuck, whether they come back), the game sends events such as "app opened", "battle started", "battle ended", "upgrade bought", "gear changed", "ad offered" and "ad watched", with details like the zone number, the result, how long a battle took and your ship's power. Each event carries a random ID made when you install the game, plus your device model, iOS version and the game's version.

The game does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any free text, and the random ID is not your device's advertising identifier. Your IP address is not stored with the gameplay events and is not used to work out where you are. Deleting the game deletes the ID; a reinstall makes a new one.

Ads. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, AdMob collects information about your device and the ad (for example, an advertising identifier if you allow tracking, your IP address and whether the ad was shown or tapped) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Who processes it

  • PostHog stores the gameplay events for us (in the United States) and only uses them to provide the analytics service.
  • Google AdMob serves the ads and processes ad data under Google's own privacy policy.

We don't sell the gameplay events.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether Black Tide may track you for ads. If you say no (or later turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • Personalized ads and "Do not sell or share". Where the law gives you these choices, the game shows Google's consent message, and your answer is applied to every ad.
  • Gameplay events. To have the events tied to your install deleted, email us from the game's support address with the approximate date you installed it.

Children

Black Tide is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.
